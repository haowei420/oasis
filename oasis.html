About

1991
Origins · Manchester

Oasis formed in Manchester, England, founded by Liam Gallagher and others. Shortly after, Liam's older brother Noel joined as lead guitarist and primary songwriter — becoming the creative soul of the band.

1994
— 95
Rise · Definitely Maybe & Morning Glory

Their debut Definitely Maybe became the fastest-selling debut album in UK history. The 1995 follow-up (What's the Story) Morning Glory? was their masterpiece — "Wonderwall" and "Don't Look Back in Anger" became anthems for a generation, with the album selling over 22 million copies worldwide.

1996
Peak · Knebworth & Britpop

The Knebworth concerts drew over 250,000 fans across two nights — widely regarded as one of the greatest live events in rock history. At the height of Britpop, the legendary rivalry between Oasis and Blur defined an era of British pop culture.

2009
→ 25
Split · Reunion

The band split in 2009 following an explosive falling-out between the Gallagher brothers. But in 2024, Noel and Liam announced a reunion — and in 2025, Oasis returned with a long-awaited world tour.

Members

Liam Gallagher
Lead Vocals

Known for his iconic hands-clasped stance and razor-sharp voice, Liam's magnetic stage presence made him one of the most iconic frontmen in rock history. Born 1972, Manchester.

Noel Gallagher
Lead Guitar · Songwriter

The creative genius behind Oasis. His impeccable songwriting and infectious guitar work produced rock classics that defined an entire generation. Born 1967, Manchester.

Paul "Bonehead" Arthurs
Rhythm Guitar · Back for 2025

A founding member whose solid rhythm guitar laid the foundation of the Oasis sound. After departing in 1999, he reunited with Liam on his solo tours and returned for the 2025 Oasis reunion.

Gem Archer
Guitar · Back for 2025

Joined Oasis in 2000 to replace Bonehead, having previously fronted Heavy Stereo. His versatile playing added new textures to the band's later albums and he returns as part of the 2025 reunion lineup. Born 1966, Sunderland.

Andy Bell
Bass · Back for 2025

Former frontman of shoegaze legends Ride, Andy joined Oasis on bass in 1999 after McGuigan's departure. His musical depth and energy helped shape the band's sound through to their 2009 split — and he's back for the 2025 reunion. Born 1970, Cardiff.

Paul McGuigan
Bass · 1991–1999

Nicknamed "Guigsy", the founding bassist of Oasis. His understated style and solid basslines provided the backbone of the band's early sound.

Tony McCarroll
Drums · 1991–1995

Part of Oasis from the very beginning, his drumming appears on Definitely Maybe. Departed in 1995 and was replaced by Alan White.

Alan White
Drums · 1995–2004

His powerful drumming left a lasting mark on Morning Glory and Be Here Now, making him a core part of Oasis's classic lineup during their golden era.

Zak Starkey
Drums · 2004–2008

Son of Ringo Starr and a powerhouse behind the kit, Zak brought raw energy to Oasis during the Don't Believe the Truth and Dig Out Your Soul era. He also played alongside The Who throughout his career. Born 1965, London.

Chris Sharrock
Drums · 2008–2009

A veteran of The La's and World Party, Chris stepped in as drummer for the final chapter of Oasis before the 2009 split. He later became a fixture in Liam Gallagher's solo band. Born 1964, Liverpool.
